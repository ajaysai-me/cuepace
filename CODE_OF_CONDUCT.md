# Code of Conduct

CuePace is built by people with different backgrounds, skill levels and ways of working. We want everyone who takes part to feel welcome and safe.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Be specific and constructive.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and say so when you've made a mistake.
- Respect people's time, privacy and boundaries.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments of any kind, including about identity, appearance, ability, experience or background.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or personal and political attacks.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.

## Where this applies

Everywhere the project lives: issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and any other space where you represent CuePace.

## Reporting

If something happens that makes you or someone else uncomfortable, report it privately to the maintainers through the repository's private security/contact options, or by opening a minimal issue asking a maintainer to reach out. Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban anyone who breaks this code of conduct. We'll always try to talk first and act proportionately.
